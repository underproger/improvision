An entrance that works for your brand

An entrance creates the first impression of a venue before the customer even opens the door. The portal, storefront, sign and lighting must work as one and withstand daily foot traffic and weather.

We design entrances to the chain’s brand book, manufacture the metal structures and cladding in our own workshop and install them with our own crews — including approvals with the mall, the city or the building owner.

What’s included

  • Facade measurements and entrance design
  • Metal frames, portals and opening surrounds
  • Approval of the facade passport and design with the mall
  • Glazing, glass doors and automatic systems
  • Cladding in composite, metal or porcelain stoneware
  • Signage, lighting and turnkey installation

For chain clients we develop standard solutions that scale easily: a design approved once is adapted to different openings and facades.

The frame, cladding, glazing and sign are made and installed by one team — no gaps between contractors.

Structures are calculated for wind and snow loads, and fixings are designed for the specific substrate. That’s why the entrance stays geometrically accurate and safe for years.

“The entrance is the face of a venue. It should be recognisable from afar and flawless up close.”

After installation we provide a warranty on structures and lighting and, if needed, service maintenance: module replacement, automation repairs and graphics updates.

Work stages

  1. 01

    Measurements

    Site visit, measurements and facade photo survey.

  2. 02

    Design

    Design, drawings, approvals and estimate.

  3. 03

    Production

    Frame, cladding and sign made in our own workshop.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Mounting, connection and turnkey handover.
