A modern facade solution — aesthetics, energy efficiency and durability

A ventilated facade is a rainscreen system with an air gap between the insulation and the cladding. This removes moisture, improves the building’s thermal insulation and allows any architectural idea to be realised.

improvision delivers the full cycle: from site survey and subframe design to element production and turnkey installation. We work with certified systems and take responsibility for the result.

What’s included

  • Site survey and measurements
  • Manufacturing of elements in our own workshop
  • Subframe design and
    panel layout
  • Installation of subframe, insulation
    and cladding
  • Calculation of fixings
    and thermal performance
  • Handover and warranty
    service

“Turnkey” is a model in which the client receives a finished result rather than a set of separate services. The contractor is responsible for the whole chain: design, materials, production, installation and handover. The client doesn’t need to coordinate several crews and align their schedules.
